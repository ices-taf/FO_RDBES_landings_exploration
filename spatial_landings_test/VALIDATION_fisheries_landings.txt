FISHERIESXPLORER — VALIDATION RECORD
19 October 2026

SCOPE

This record distinguishes checks against the supplied HTML aggregates from
checks of the new R calculations using controlled synthetic data. The original
extendedRDBES.RData, SID RDS, and the fisheriesXplorer application source were
not available for an end-to-end run of the user's production analysis or app.

SUPPLIED HTML AND PREVIEW

The supplied map contains 120 rectangle symbols and six displayed categories:
HOM, PIL, WHB, ANE, MAC and OTH. Its embedded chart widths have a maximum of
15 pixels and a median of approximately 2.77 pixels; 78 of 120 are narrower
than 5 pixels. These measurements describe the stored configuration, not a
claim that the original plugin dynamically changes its CSS size when zooming.

The preview preserves every original rectangle/category value. The sum is
214,838,934.76166666 in unspecified source units. JavaScript summation gives
214,838,934.76166674 because of floating-point summation order; no data adjustment
was made. UI display rounding and this machine precision difference do not
change exported source values.

The original coordinates cover the Biscay/Iberian extent. The source HTML does
not store the requested years, weight unit or the members of its original OTH
category. The preview explicitly labels these limits. The original polygon
boundary was repaired and its polygon parts retained so a stray point fragment
did not become a default Leaflet pin.

With a Natural Earth 1:50m land mask, 23 display centres move into the marine
part of their own reporting rectangle. Rectangle 15E2 has no marine display
geometry under that mask. Its 1,363 source units (WHB 336, MAC 1,027) remain in
the total, dropdown selection, raw outline and CSV. No quantity is apportioned
by water area or moved between rectangles.

BROWSER CHECKS: STANDALONE AND R-GENERATED HTML

The same interaction checks passed on both the delivered standalone preview
and an HTML file generated through render_fisheries_landings() followed by
save_fisheries_map() in R.

  - Initial state contains 120 reporting rectangles and the expected total.
  - No broken-image default map markers are present.
  - The rectangle selector contains the regional overview and all 120 cells.
  - Composition view draws non-overlapping equal-sized donut symbols.
  - A selected coastal donut is exactly 34 by 34 CSS pixels before and after
    zooming to level 8. The selected cell keeps priority when other donuts are
    omitted by the collision check.
  - The moved anchor for 15E8 exposes its coastal-placement note.
  - Species view switches to PIL and updates the legend to Sardine.
  - Grid toggle and scroll-wheel zoom switch change their respective states.
  - Selecting 15E2 retains and displays its 1,363 source units and geometry flag.
  - CSV download in composition view produces 720 data rows (120 x 6), despite
    a single-cell selection, and includes units and rectangle identifiers.
  - Reinitializing the same map leaves one interface shell and does not grow
    the map's layer count. A selected-cell overlay is removed on reset.
  - At a 390-pixel viewport there is no horizontal document overflow and the
    details panel is below the map.
  - A 550-pixel-wide container on a desktop viewport also stacks the panel.
  - No browser JavaScript errors or remote network requests occur with the
    default offline map. Requests were blocked during the interaction checks.

Visual inspection also covered the default landings view, composition view,
coastal selection and the mobile layout. Overlap thinning varies with available
map area: at the initial desktop test extent the standalone file drew 48 of 119
eligible marine symbols; the R widget's shorter viewport drew 28. This affects
symbol visibility only, not quantities or selectable rectangles.

Three further client scenarios passed: all-null values stay unavailable; genuine
zeros display as zero with undefined shares; and empty species lists disable
Composition/Species while retaining rectangle selection. None creates a donut
or throws a JavaScript exception. Each rerender leaves one interface shell.

R DATA PREPARATION CHECKS

Controlled fixtures verified:

  - An intentionally large 2021 record is excluded from a 2022–2023 request.
  - Kilograms are converted to tonnes once.
  - Species use a common rectangle-year denominator: MAC values of 1,000 kg
    and 3,000 kg average to 2 t; PIL with 2,000 kg only in the first year of
    the same represented rectangle averages to 1 t over both years.
  - An explicit missing weight excludes that year for all selected species.
  - Strict policy keeps a cell with a missing selected year unknown.
  - Available policy preserves a usable zero year and reports one usable year
    when the other year is absent.
  - Zero policy can include absent years as zero but never changes explicit
    missing weights into zero.
  - coverage=TRUE permits a declared complete no-record year to be zero;
    coverage=FALSE excludes a year despite available numerical records.
  - The species crossing a percentage target is retained: weights 85 and 15
    with a target of 80 retain the first separately, keep total 100, and give
    Other a share of 0.15.
  - ANK=10 and MON=20 recode to ANF=30 before lookup and aggregation.
  - A conflicting Pelagic/Demersal lookup assignment raises an error.
  - Under available policy, a year invalidated by one species is excluded from
    every species denominator in that rectangle.
  - Two explicit missing source rows are recorded as two missing rows in the
    annual diagnostics.
  - A wholly unpublishable region and its legend totals remain unknown.
  - Empty source data for a specified grid gives an unknown total under strict
    policy and an empty species legend.

R GEOMETRY CHECKS

Synthetic land and three reporting rectangles with totals 10, 20 and 0 verified
that a land midpoint moves into its own water polygon, a wholly land-covered
cell has no display anchor, and a valid marine midpoint stays in place. All
three raw rectangle features survive, and the total remains 30.

The hidden feature retains an explicit null geometry key. A manual anchor on
land is rejected; a valid same-cell marine override is accepted and recorded.
Centre coordinates inconsistent with their ICES rectangle ID are rejected.
Empty prepared data for a known rectangle can still produce a geometry payload.
One-year and one-member metadata remain arrays in JSON.

Focused repaired-boundary fixtures verified Polygon+Point collections become
polygon outlines; ordinary lines survive; LineString+Point collections become
line outlines; separate valid polygon and line features remain; and point-only
boundaries fail clearly. R named colour royalblue becomes #4169E1, lowercase hex
becomes uppercase hex, and species-code alignment is preserved.

SERIALIZATION AND R COMPATIBILITY

The final R source embeds its complete client JavaScript and CSS. The wrapper
was exercised with R 4.3.3, dplyr 1.1.4, tidyr 1.3.1, jsonlite 1.8.8, sf 1.0.15,
htmltools 0.5.7, htmlwidgets 1.6.4 and leaflet 2.2.3. The sf runtime used GEOS
3.12.1, GDAL 3.8.4 and PROJ 9.4.0. The browser was Chromium 155.0.8059.84.

The standalone preview includes Leaflet JavaScript 1.9.4. The tested R package
export uses its bundled Leaflet JavaScript 1.3.1; the same client passed on both.

Normalizing nested GeoJSON coordinates to numeric matrices reduced the tested
coordinate normalization plus serialization step to approximately 7.7 seconds,
compared with approximately 111 seconds for the original deeply nested list
serialization in this environment. These are development-environment timings,
not a promise about production application latency.

Comparison after JSON.parse covered 159,162 numeric values plus every object
member and array shape in the preview payload. With digits=17 there were zero
differences. Fixtures covered all GeoJSON geometry families, null geometries,
2D/3D coordinates, holes, singleton sequences and repeated normalization.

The example script was parsed and sourced. Lookup normalization, the REB rule,
missing-column error and StockKeyLabel prefix handling were verified. A stubbed
two-guild export-loop check verified every forwarded argument against the
actual wrapper and preparation signatures, including years and guild overrides.
That forwarding check did not download data or produce production maps.

A final integration check sourced the assembled deliverable and passed synthetic
source records through plot_fisheries_landings(), export_fisheries_calculations()
and save_fisheries_map(). Three rectangles over three years produced two display
categories and a 9-tonne regional total, with the expected common denominators
and normalized colour. Seven calculation files and a self-contained HTML widget
were written successfully.

LIMITS

These checks establish the implemented behaviour for the supplied aggregate
preview and controlled calculation fixtures. They do not establish source units,
the fleet-expansion interpretation of the _perVessel field, complete country or
fleet reporting, the original years, or original OTH membership. They do not
validate the result of the new annual averaging on the unavailable source records.

The Shiny integration is supplied as a compatible renderLeaflet/leafletOutput
recipe. Saved R-widget rendering and client reinitialization were tested; a live
fisheriesXplorer deployment, its authentication, its content security policy and
its host application's event/reactive behaviour were not tested or changed.
Safari and Firefox were not part of this browser run. Coastal placement is only
as detailed as the supplied land mask and guarantees the anchor point, not the
entire circular glyph footprint at every zoom level.
